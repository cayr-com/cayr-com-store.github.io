GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you should know before using the catalog.

Are these final CAYR-COM selling prices?

No. The amounts shown are dated published reference prices in USD. Confirm actual pricing, stock, taxes, shipping, and availability with the store.

Can I pay directly through the website?

Not yet. This version is a catalog and inquiry demo. No orders, cards, or payments are collected by this website.

How do I ask about a phone?

Add one or more phones to your inquiry list and copy the summary. Once business contact details are verified and configured, share that summary with the store.

Why do some products show illustrations?

Where a model-specific image cannot be provided with sufficient certainty, a clearly marked illustration is shown instead of an incorrect photo.
